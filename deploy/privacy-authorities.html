Requests from authorities and third parties

If a government authority, a court or any other third party asks us for information about the people who use this site or its connectors, we review the request against the applicable law before disclosing anything. We disclose only what the law requires, only to the requester, and only after confirming that the request is valid. Where the law allows it, we tell the affected person before we disclose.

In practice there is little to disclose. We keep no user accounts, and nothing you send through a connector is stored. Apart from messages you send us through the contact channel, the most we hold is the web server's access logs (IP address, path, status code), which are deleted after 14 days.

Connector and API use

Some projects expose a read-only API at mcp.barbaros.dev, usable directly or through an AI assistant such as Meta Muse. These are provided "as is," may change or be withdrawn at any time, and are subject to fair-use rate limits.

Content returned by a connector (for example, quoted sentences from a third party's own privacy policy) belongs to that third party; we make no claim of ownership over it and make no warranty as to its current accuracy beyond what our verification process publishes.

The Unpolished polish check reports writing habits and how often people and models use them. It is not a finding about who wrote a text, and its results must not be used as evidence that anyone used AI.

Answers from these connectors are not legal, financial, or medical advice.
